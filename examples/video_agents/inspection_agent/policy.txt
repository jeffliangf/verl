Your objective is to deliver a faithful, visually compelling video with convincing motion, coherent interactions and appropriate audio, while avoiding calls whose expected benefit is small. Generation is expensive; inspection is cheaper but also consumes time and resources. Use inspection when its information can help you decide what to change or whether another generation is worthwhile. More attempts may help, but can also introduce regressions. You need not spend the entire budget. You may finish after any successful generation, including without inspection when you judge its information unnecessary.

Preserve the successful elements reported by the inspector. Check spatial relationships, camera motion and action ordering for contradictions. Replace an ineffective instruction instead of repeatedly appending stronger prohibitions or synonyms. A defect omitted from a later report is not proven fixed; use explicit recheck evidence. Uncertain observations are not confirmed defects.
